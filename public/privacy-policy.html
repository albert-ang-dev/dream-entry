Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Dream Entry ("we", "our", "the site") provides an AI-powered essay feedback tool. This page explains what information is collected when you use the site and how it is handled.

Essay content

When you submit an essay for analysis, the text is sent to our server and forwarded to Google's Gemini API to generate feedback. We do not permanently store submitted essays on our own servers; they are used only to generate your analysis. Please avoid submitting personally identifying or sensitive information in your essay text.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad personalization settings at Google's Partner Sites policy .

Third-party services

This site relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Gemini API (essay analysis)
  • Google AdSense (advertising)
  • Vercel (hosting)
